# Competitive position and pitch discipline

## The category

Professional collision reconstruction and video forensics already exist. Blackbox is a guided, evidence-first workspace for a person or advocate with a few clips, plus an expert-readable package. Its distinctive claim must be demonstrated, not asserted.

## Competitive thesis

The most defensible combination is **evidence preservation + independent statement checks + answerability analysis + active missing-evidence guidance + conditional counterfactuals + evidence-linked 3D**. None of those features alone is a monopoly. The claim is that Blackbox integrates them into a coherent case workflow for users underserved by expert tools. Check this claim again before submission; public repositories can change quickly.

## Pitch strengths

- Clear human problem and strong staged demo.
- Meta models are used for perception and speech, with deterministic checks and calculations.
- A surprising result can be shown without claiming legal fault.
- The same case can produce a preservation plan and prevention hypotheses.

## Pitch weaknesses to address

- Arbitrary dashcam speed and full collision physics may be unidentifiable.
- A generated 3D scene can be mistaken for recovered reality.
- Existing professional tools already provide much of the underlying physics.
- A prototype does not prove usability, adoption, legal acceptance, or lives saved.
- A public GitHub scan cannot establish that no private competitor exists.

## Judge-facing proof order

Show a source frame first, then the measurement and error, then the claim check, then the what-if, then the 3D explanation. Present a failure/abstention case. Publish real test counts and held-out errors. Do not say “top 1%” as a measured fact before results or judging.
