# Safety, privacy, and evidence handling

## Product stance

Blackbox is a decision-support and evidence-organization tool. It does not determine legal responsibility, prove video authenticity, identify a person, or replace a trained reconstructionist. Any external use of its estimates requires review of originals, calibration, methods, and limitations.

## Intake and consent

Only upload media the user is entitled to handle. The demo uses staged or licensed footage with consent from visible participants. Warn before uploading sensitive road footage to hosted model services. Preserve the original file separately from previews and masked share copies. Make retention and deletion controls visible to the case owner.

## Evidence integrity

Compute a hash immediately at intake and again on export. Record source acquisition notes, file metadata, and all transformations. A hash proves that the stored file has not changed since intake; it cannot prove that the file was authentic before intake. Keep a versioned audit trail for manual corrections, synchronization edits, and claim decisions.

## Statements and contested facts

Do not label a speaker a liar. A contradiction applies to a narrowly defined claim and a specific visible event. Transcription errors, translation ambiguity, incomplete viewpoints, and time-offset uncertainty can all change the result. Provide original speech, transcript version, translation, test rule, and source frames. Human reviewers can challenge and revise findings without erasing the history.

## Simulation boundaries

Counterfactual percentages are conditional fractions of modeled scenarios under stated assumptions, not probabilities that the real incident would or would not have happened. Generated 3D meshes and camera viewpoints are illustrative. A synthetic view cannot reveal an object or event hidden from every source camera. The UI must visibly mark uncertainty, occlusion, and unsupported estimates.

## Privacy controls

Private cases by default; short-lived, scoped share links; role-based access; server-side secrets; encrypted transfer; separate masked and original downloads; explicit deletion and retention policies. Blur faces and plates in public or shareable media where appropriate, but retain originals for authorized case review. Aggregated junction analysis requires consent, deduplication, minimum group sizes, and suppression of identifying trajectories.

## Misuse controls

Avoid facial identification, plate lookup, targeted tracking of people, automatic police or insurer submissions, and auto-generated blame language. Draft preservation requests for human review only. Flag source-media anomalies for expert review rather than issuing an authenticity certificate. Do not expose exact camera-location guesses publicly without verification and a legitimate reason.

## Required report notices

Every report includes: source and processing history; exact limits of the camera views; calibration and timing assumptions; model version; uncertainty and sensitivity; human corrections; synthetic-view labels; and a statement that findings are preliminary and require qualified review for consequential use.
